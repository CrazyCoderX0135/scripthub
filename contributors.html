📋 how to contribute
  • 1
    write a script — Python, JavaScript, Shell, or PowerShell. Make sure it runs cleanly and does something useful or fun.
  • 2
    hit submit — fill out the submit form with your script, a short description, and optionally your email to be credited.
  • 3
    review — the team reviews your submission for quality and safety. usually fast.
  • 4
    go live — your script appears on the site, credited to you. your name shows up on this page.
✅ guidelines
good submissions use only built-in or widely available packages · include a clear one-line description · run without errors · do something genuinely useful or fun
won't be accepted: malware, exploits, intentionally harmful or offensive code
